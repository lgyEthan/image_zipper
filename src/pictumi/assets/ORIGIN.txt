Pictumi artwork and test image

app-icon.svg: original vector drawing created for Pictumi. It depicts a photo
inside two crop corners. No Apple, Instagram, or other third-party icon assets
were copied. check.svg and chevron.svg are simple original geometric controls.

selftest.heic: original, synthetic 720 x 480 geometric landscape generated for
codec verification. Contains no photograph, person, location, or capture metadata.
The HEIC encoder is used only to create this fixture; it is not needed at runtime.
